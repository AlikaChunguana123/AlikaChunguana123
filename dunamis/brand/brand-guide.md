# Dunamis Brand Guide

## The name
**Dunamis** (δύναμις, *DOO-nah-mees*) is the Greek word for **power, strength and ability**. It's the root of words like *dynamic* and *dynamite*. The idea: every small business already has the power to grow, and Dunamis helps unlock it.

**Brand line:** "Power to grow." · **Positioning line:** "Big-company advice, small-business price."

## Brand idea
Dunamis is the friendly, practical business advisor that every small business owner deserves but could never afford. We speak plainly, give concrete next steps, and celebrate small wins.

- **Mission:** Give every small business access to expert-level advice that fits its sector, budget and reality.
- **Vision:** A world where no good business idea fails for lack of advice.
- **Personality:** Warm · Practical · Optimistic · Trustworthy · Local-minded
- **Tagline options:** "Power to grow." / "Unlock the power in your business." / "Your business advisor in your pocket."

## Logo
| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo.svg` | Primary logo (mark + wordmark + descriptor) for website, documents, signage |
| `logo-mark.svg` | App icon, social avatar, favicon, stickers |

**Meaning:** The speech bubble is the consultation. The sprout growing inside is your business growing with power (dunamis). The amber sun is the bright idea.

**Rules:**
- Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the sprout's leaves.
- Minimum size: 24 px tall for the mark, 120 px wide for the full logo.
- On dark backgrounds use the mark with a Cream (#FAF7F0) wordmark.
- Don't stretch, rotate, recolour, add shadows or put it on a busy photo.

## Colour palette
| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deep Teal** (primary) | `#0F4C5C` | 15, 76, 92 | Logo, headers, navigation. Trust & expertise |
| **Sprout Green** (secondary) | `#3FB27F` | 63, 178, 127 | Growth, success states, highlights |
| **Sunrise Amber** (accent) | `#F2A541` | 242, 165, 65 | Buttons, calls-to-action, "idea" moments. Use sparingly |
| **Cream** (background) | `#FAF7F0` | 250, 247, 240 | Page backgrounds, cards on dark |
| **Ink** (text) | `#1B2631` | 27, 38, 49 | Body text |
| **Mist** (neutral) | `#E3EBEA` | 227, 235, 234 | Borders, dividers, subtle panels |

Ratio: ~60% Cream/white, 25% Deep Teal, 10% Sprout Green, 5% Sunrise Amber.
Accessibility: Ink on Cream and Cream on Deep Teal both pass WCAG AA for body text. Put Ink text on Amber buttons, never white text.

## Typography
- **Headings:** Poppins (Bold 700 / SemiBold 600), free on Google Fonts
- **Body & UI:** Inter (Regular 400 / Medium 500), free on Google Fonts
- Fallback: system sans-serif (Segoe UI, Roboto, Arial)

## Voice & tone
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Try a loyalty card: buy 9 coffees, get the 10th free." | "Leverage customer retention synergies." |
| Short sentences, real numbers, clear next steps | Jargon, long theory, vague advice |
| Encourage: "Great start, here's how to grow it." | Judge: "You're doing it wrong." |
| Respect tight budgets: give free or low-cost options first | Assume big budgets |

## Imagery
Real small business owners (market stalls, salons, workshops, farms, cafés), natural light, diverse people, hands at work. Illustrations: simple flat shapes using the palette with rounded corners. Avoid stock-photo suits and handshakes.

## Applications
- **App icon:** `logo-mark.svg` on Cream or Deep Teal, rounded square
- **Social posts:** Cream background, Deep Teal headline, one Amber highlight word
- **Business cards / flyers:** Deep Teal front with Cream logo, Cream back with contact details in Ink
- **Merch:** Deep Teal T-shirts or tote bags with the Cream mark
