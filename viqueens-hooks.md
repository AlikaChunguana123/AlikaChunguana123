The plot: two Viking girls travel **Norway → China** to return a stowaway, and learn **dragon kites, fireworks and Kung Fu**, with a theme of **friendship**.
**Don't claim anything beyond that.** No release dates, box office, "Haaland's acting debut", song titles, or "biggest movie of the year" unless the campaign team confirms it.

Hook text must be showing in the first frame, in big readable text, placed in the middle third of the screen so the app's buttons don't cover it.

## 📊 HOOK FORMULAS (to make your own)

- **Question/reveal:** "Guess who voices her 👀" → name revealed on screen
- **Comparison joke:** "We got ___ before GTA VI 😭"
- **Engagement:** "Rate this 1–10 👇" or "Tag a City fan"

## Testing tips
- Make **3 versions of the same Haaland clip** with different hooks (e.g. #1, #3, #13). Keep the one that performs best and remix it.
- Use a mix of about **50% Haaland**, 25% Rita Ora/soundtrack and 25% trailer moments (the brief asks for a mix).
- Cut any dead frames at the start. The clip should start moving in frame 1.
- After uploading, check that the clip is live and displays correctly on all 3 platforms.
