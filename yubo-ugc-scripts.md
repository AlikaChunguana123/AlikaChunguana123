# Yubo Creator Scripts: Instagram Reels only (1-month campaign)

**Hard rules for every video (from the brief):**
- [ ] Mention **Yubo only**. Never name or show a competitor (Huzz, Wizz, etc.), even in a "compared to…" joke.
- [ ] **No dating language.** Yubo is for **making friends / hanging out / meeting people**. Avoid: dating, date, match, crush, rizz, flirt, single, "find someone", hearts/kissing emojis. Use: friends, squad, hang out, vibe, chat, go live, meet new people.
- [ ] **Don't make it look like an ad.** No "sponsored by" scripted tone, no sales pitch, no "download now!!" ending. Film it like you're just sharing something you use.
- [ ] **No minors anywhere**: not you as a kid, not memes with kids, not friends or siblings under 18, not other users' faces in screen recordings.
- [ ] **Blur every other user's face and username** in screen recordings (privacy, and you can't verify their age).
- [ ] Instagram only. Put the Instagram Sideshift link in your IG bio.
- [ ] The campaign is aimed at **US creators**. If you're not in the US, check with the campaign before posting.

> Before filming, use Yubo yourself and only show features you actually see in the app (swiping to add friends, live streams, chats, etc.).

## Tips for keeping it organic (not ad-like)
- Film on your phone in your real spaces. No studio look, no scripted reading tone.
- Mention Yubo **once, naturally**, mid-video. Don't open with the brand name.
- End on a joke or a question, not "download Yubo".
- Use trending Reels audio for the skits (#1, #3, #5).
- 3–5 hashtags: #yubo plus niche ones (#makingfriends #introvert #gaming #relatable).
- Turn on Instagram's "Paid partnership" label if the campaign requires it. Being organic in style doesn't remove disclosure rules.
- Before posting, re-read the on-screen text and caption for any dating words or competitor names.
