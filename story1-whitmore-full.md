- **Sound effects:** champagne pop (sc. 4), cash register "cha-ching" (sc. 6), paper rustle (sc. 7), teacup clink (sc. 10), gavel hit when **$0** appears (sc. 9).
- **Number cards:** a white card with a big bold number. Green for income, red for spending and for $0. Animate the crossed-out line drawing across $40M.
- **Captions:** white text in a black rounded box, in the bottom third. Keep it clear of Instagram's buttons.
