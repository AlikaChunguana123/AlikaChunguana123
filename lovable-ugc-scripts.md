# Lovable UGC Scripts: TikTok + Instagram (new account, 5 posts/week)

**Rules for every video (from the brief):**
- [ ] Good lighting (face a window, or put a ring light behind the camera)
- [ ] Longer than 5 seconds (aim for 15–35s)
- [ ] Text in the **default TikTok font**, white or white with a black outline
- [ ] **NEVER say you earned money.** Say "you *can* make money", "you *could* charge", "this *could* be a side hustle". No "I made $X", no fake payment screenshots, no income numbers presented as yours.
- [ ] English only, and cross-post every video to both TikTok and Instagram (export without the TikTok watermark for Reels)

**Filming the build:** screen-record the Lovable build. If you use green screen, film yourself in front of the recording. Speed up the "generating" part (2–4x) so the site appears fast. Always end on the **finished, working site**: scroll it, click a button, open it on your phone.

> Only show and say what Lovable actually does in your own account. Try each build before filming it.

> **These are my own scripts.** Fluencify will send proven formats and example videos, so follow theirs first and use these to fill gaps or adapt them.

---

## Warm-up (days 1–4, before branded posts)
Fluencify covers this part, so follow their instructions first. If you're left to do it yourself:
- **Every day:** spend 20–30 min watching and liking tech / AI tools / side hustle / no-code content. Follow 10–15 creators in the niche and leave a few real comments.
- **Profile:** a clear photo, a niche bio (e.g. "building websites with AI, no code 💻"), the Sideshift link in the bio (TikTok link → TikTok bio, Instagram link → Instagram bio).
- **Days 3–4:** optionally post 1–2 unbranded niche videos (e.g. "3 AI tools that feel illegal to know", or a day-in-the-life desk setup) so the account doesn't look empty.

## WEEK 2

### 6. "Things you can build with Lovable (that aren't just websites)" (listicle, green screen)
**~25s**: show 3 quick builds: a habit tracker app, a quiz, a link-in-bio page. Hook: **"you can build APPS with this??"** End: "Which one should I build in full?"

### 7. "Rating websites I built with AI in under 5 minutes" (series)
**~25s**: show 3 builds, rate each /10 on screen, and make one funny fail on purpose (e.g. a weird prompt). Hook: **"rating websites AI built for me in under 5 minutes"**.

### 8. "POV: you finally build the app idea you've had for 3 years" (POV + humour)
**~20s**: you staring at an old notes app idea → type it into Lovable → it exists → dramatic music, you staring at the ceiling. Text: **"POV: you finally build the app idea you've been talking about since 2022"**.

### 9. "Comment a business, I'll build its website" (engagement series)
**~20s**: hook **"comment any business and I'll build its website with AI"**. Then reply to comments with video replies, each one a new post. This is easy content for a new account.

### 10. "How to make money with AI as a beginner (honest version)" (side-hustle, compliant)
**~30s** | Talking head
- Hook: **"the honest way beginners could make money with AI website builders"**
- Points: 1) learn to build with Lovable (show build) 2) make 3 sample sites for practice 3) offer to build sites for local businesses or creators.
- Close: "No guarantees, it takes effort, but the skill is free to start learning."
- ⚠️ Say "could" and "can". No income claims, no "I make $X a month".

## Tips
1. The hook text should be showing in the **first frame**.
2. Always show the **finished, working site**. That's the payoff people stay for.
3. Use 3–5 niche hashtags: #lovable #aitools #nocode #sidehustle #techtok. Don't use #fyp spam.
4. Reply to comments with video replies. That gives you free extra posts.
5. Turn on TikTok's "Branded content" / Instagram's "Paid partnership" label if the campaign requires it.
6. Re-check the **income rule** before every post: captions, on-screen text and voiceover all count.
