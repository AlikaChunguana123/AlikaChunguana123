# MIA — "The Power of Forgiveness"
## Shot-by-shot prompt pack for Seedance / AI Playground

These tools generate **one short shot per prompt** (≈5s) and have **no memory
between generations**. That is why a single long prompt returns a 5-second
montage instead of a 70-second film.

Method: paste **BLOCK A** at the top of every generation, then add **one shot
line** underneath. Twelve shots ≈ 60–72 seconds.

Set the aspect ratio to **9:16 in the tool's settings** — asking for it in the
prompt text does not work. Lock the **seed** if the tool offers one, and reuse
the same seed for every shot.

---

## BLOCK A — paste this above every shot

3D animated children's film, cinematic Pixar-quality rendering, warm golden
sunlight, soft saturated colours, expressive facial animation, gentle camera
movement, wholesome and emotionally warm, never frightening.

MIA: an 8-year-old Black girl, warm brown skin, big expressive brown eyes,
curly black hair in two small puffs with colourful hair clips, yellow cardigan
over a white shirt, denim overalls, pink sneakers.

NOAH: her younger brother, 5 years old, Black, short curly black hair, light
blue t-shirt, denim shorts, white sneakers.

JESUS: a gentle adult man, long brown hair, short beard, warm brown eyes,
simple cream robe with a light blue shawl, peaceful and loving expression.

Keep every character's face, hair, clothing and proportions identical.
No morphing, no distorted hands or faces, no text or lettering on screen,
no scary imagery, no violence.

## THE TWELVE SHOTS

**1 · 6s** — Interior, sunlit living room. Mia sits at a small wooden table,
tongue between her teeth, colouring a bright drawing. Slow push-in on her happy
face. Warm morning light through the window.

**2 · 5s** — Noah runs past the table and his elbow catches a glass of water.
The glass tips. Close-up, slow motion, the water arcs out.

**3 · 5s** — Top-down shot of the drawing as the water spreads and the colours
bleed and run. Mia's hands enter frame too late.

**4 · 5s** — Mia's face, close-up. Her smile collapses into anger. She stands
up sharply. Noah, behind her, small and frightened.

**5 · 6s** — Noah looks up at Mia, eyes wet, shoulders drawn in. Mia turns her
back to him and walks out of frame. He is left alone with the ruined paper.

**6 · 6s** — Exterior, garden. Mia sits alone on a low step, arms crossed tight,
staring at the grass. The light is cooler and greyer than before. Leaves move
in the wind.

**7 · 6s** — Jesus quietly sits down on the step beside her. He does not speak.
He simply looks out at the garden with her. The light warms very slightly.
Mia glances at him, uncertain.

**8 · 6s** — Jesus gestures gently toward a small school backpack on the grass
beside Mia. Mia grips the strap with both hands and pulls. The backpack does
not move. She strains, surprised at its weight.

**9 · 6s** — The backpack lies open. Inside are three smooth coloured stones —
one red, one green, one purple. Close-up, shallow depth of field, warm rim
light on the stones. *(Leave the stones blank — see the note below.)*

**10 · 6s** — Mia looks from the stones to Jesus. He speaks gently, one hand
open. She listens, her jaw softening. Over-the-shoulder, warm afternoon light.

**11 · 6s** — Mia walks across the grass toward Noah, who sits hugging his
knees. She crouches to his level. He looks up. She smiles softly and hugs him.
Golden sunlight floods the garden.

**12 · 7s** — Wide shot: Mia and Noah sit together on the grass drawing on a
fresh sheet of paper, laughing. The empty backpack lies flat and light beside
them. Far behind, Jesus watches, smiles, and the camera slowly rises.

---

## Why the stones should be blank

In your 5-second test the AI wrote **"ANGET"** on the yellow stone — misspelled,
and a fourth stone you never asked for. These models cannot spell reliably.

Generate the stones plain, then the words **ANGER · HURT · RESENTMENT** get
added afterwards in editing, where they will be correct every time.
The same goes for the closing card:

> *"Forgiveness doesn't change what happened.
> But sometimes, it changes what happens next."*
>
> **"Don't carry yesterday's hurt into tomorrow."**
