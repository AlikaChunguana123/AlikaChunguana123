# Simone | Money Mindset: animated old-money story prompts

**Format (matches your reference videos):** about 45–50s vertical 9:16 animated story.
- Simone narrates from her chair.
- The video cuts to cartoon story scenes, with Simone in a small **circle badge** in the corner.
- A short caption sits in a black rounded box at the bottom of each scene.
- Big **number cards** (red numbers, crossed-out old values) appear at key moments.
- Every story ends on **one lesson**.

**Your bio's promise:** *"The five mistakes that kill fortunes."* Stories 1–5 are those five mistakes (fortunes that failed). Stories 6–8 are old-money families that did it right.

> All families and numbers are **fictional** (Simone's "family friends"). Don't present them as real people. Your profile is already labelled AI-generated, which is good, so keep it on.

## Caption + posting tips
- **Caption:** *Mistake #1 of 5 that kill fortunes 🫖 Which one are you making? #oldmoney #moneymindset #wealth #financialliteracy #generationalwealth*
- Post the 5 mistakes as a **numbered series** (#1–#5) and pin #1 on your profile. That matches your bio line "↓ The five mistakes that kill fortunes".
- Put the hook text on the **first frame**, then cut to the first scene within 1s.
- Voice: calm, slow, a little amused, a classy "rich auntie". Use the same AI voice for every video so it stays consistent.
- Keep it educational. Don't promise anyone will get rich, and don't give specific investment advice (e.g. "buy X").
