# Paperpal UGC Scripts: TikTok + Instagram (new account)

**Checklist before you post each video (from the brief):**
- [ ] Good lighting (face a window, or put a ring light behind the camera)
- [ ] Longer than 5 seconds (aim for 15–30s)
- [ ] Text in the **default TikTok font**, white or white with a black outline
- [ ] Paperpal shown **on another device** (laptop or monitor in frame, filmed with your phone; not just a screen recording pasted in)
- [ ] Stutters and mistakes cut out, with quick cuts (no shot over about 3s without a change)

**Filming the demo:** film the laptop screen with your phone, close up, with the screen brightness turned up. Zoom the browser to 125–150% so the text is easy to read on a phone. Each demo should show three things: the bad sentence, clicking Paperpal, and the better sentence.

> Before filming, check each feature in your own Paperpal account and say only what you see it do on screen.

## Script 1: "My supervisor's comments" (POV + humour)
**Length:** ~20s | **Format:** POV skit → screen demo

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Audio / VO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | You stare at the laptop, horrified, hand over your mouth | **POV: your supervisor returned your draft with 47 comments** | (trending sound or silence) |
| 2–5s | Close-up of the screen: a paragraph covered in comments or red marks | "unclear" "too informal" "rephrase" | "Every single one says 'too informal'…" |

| 12–17s | Before/after split: zoom on the old sentence, then the new one | **before → after** | "Same idea, it just sounds like I know what I'm doing." |
| 17–20s | You lean back and sip coffee, smug | **revision 2 hits different** | "Supervisor, see you Monday." |

**Caption:** the "too informal" comment haunted me for weeks 😭 #phdlife #academicwriting #gradschool #thesis #paperpal

## Script 4: "Rate my sentence" (before/after game)
**Length:** ~20s | **Format:** interactive and highly repeatable, so you can make it a series

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Audio / VO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Laptop screen showing one clunky sentence | **would your professor accept this sentence? 🤔** | "Would your professor accept this?" |

| 6–14s | Click Paperpal, the suggestion appears, accept it | **Paperpal version 👇** | "Here's what Paperpal turns it into." |
| 14–20s | Read the improved version and react | **comment your worst sentence and I'll fix it** | "Drop your worst sentence in the comments and I'll run it through." |

**Caption:** comment your most chaotic sentence 👇 #academicwriting #grammar #university #essay #paperpal

> The CTA at the end gets people commenting, and you can reply to those comments with video replies. That's an easy way to keep posting on a new account.

## Tips for a new account
1. **Warm up the account first.** For 2–3 days before your first post, scroll and engage with #studytok / #phdlife / #academicwriting content for about 20 minutes a day so the algorithm learns your niche.
2. **Post the same video natively on both apps.** Export it without a TikTok watermark before uploading to Instagram Reels.
3. **Start with Scripts 1, 4 and 5.** They have the strongest hooks and need the least talking.
4. **Space posts out.** 1 post a day is better than 3 at once. Each post earns for 14 days, so posting consistently stacks your earnings.
5. **Hook in the first second.** The on-screen text should already be showing in the first frame, not fade in.
6. **Keep 3–5 hashtags.** Use the niche ones above. Don't use #fyp spam.
7. **Use any required ad disclosure.** Turn on TikTok's "Branded content" toggle / Instagram's "Paid partnership" label if the campaign requires it.
